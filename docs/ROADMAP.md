# Roadmap

1. **Foundation (current):** repository, API, database/migrations, frontend, local containers, provider contracts, quality gates.
2. **Identity and reviews:** local authentication, organizations, memberships/RBAC, reviews and review members, tenant tests.
3. **Workflow and provenance:** persisted workflows/jobs/events, checkpoints, audit/provenance/AI-run registries.
4. **Protocol and search:** immutable structured protocols, canonical search concepts, provider fixtures/translators.
5. **Records:** RIS/BibTeX/CSV import, article/source model, layered non-destructive deduplication.
6. **Screening and documents:** reviewer queues/conflicts, PDF storage/validation, GROBID adapter, full-text decisions, study families.
7. **Extraction and verification:** versioned typed schemas, evidence locations, manual then mock-AI extraction, conflict/adjudication.
8. **PRISMA and export:** database-derived flow counts and portable CSV/XLSX/RIS/JSON exports.
9. **Scientific extensions:** configurable risk-of-bias tools, isolated deterministic R/metafor analysis, certainty and reporting.

Each milestone must meet the definition of done in the master specification before progression.
