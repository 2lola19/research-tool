# Product

The product is an AI-native systematic-review and meta-analysis workspace whose differentiator is governed, traceable evidence synthesis rather than generic chat with documents.

The initial MVP covers accounts and organizations, review projects, immutable protocols, canonical search strategies, citation import and non-destructive deduplication, two-stage screening, document storage, typed extraction with evidence provenance, computed PRISMA counts, export, and append-only audit history.

Success means a researcher can always see current workflow state, required attention, completed work, and the source of every consequential scientific record.
